MARTINA PIUS MINDE — FINAL PORTFOLIO
===================================

This website includes the portfolio page, original styling, navigation, and all 71 graphic design images supplied in the design archive.

SECTIONS
- Home
- About Me
- Graphic Design: logo and visual identity, social media, and print design collection
- IT Services
- Teaching
- Projects
- Contact

VIEW THE WEBSITE
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the martina-portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html to view the website in a browser.

PUBLISH ONLINE
Upload the complete contents of the martina-portfolio folder to your web hosting service. Keep the assets/graphic-design folder and all image files alongside index.html.

CONTACT
Email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Instagram links are included in the Contact section. The CV button lets visitors request your CV by email.

Gallery update: the 71 designs are shown in an equal-sized horizontal slider. Use the left/right arrow buttons to move through the gallery. Design services include logos and visual identity, company profiles, brochures and flyers, social media design, print marketing, and custom projects. Additional samples are available on request.
